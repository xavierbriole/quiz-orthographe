1. Who we are

Quiz Langues is a personal tool operated by Xavier Briole. It is used only with the operator's own TikTok accounts.

2. Data we access

When the operator connects one of his TikTok accounts, the service receives through TikTok Login Kit:

  • basic account information provided by the user.info.basic scope (such as the account's open ID);
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens allowing the service to upload videos as drafts (video.upload scope).

The service does not access any other TikTok user's data, followers, messages, comments or analytics.

3. How data is used

These tokens are used for one purpose only: uploading the generated quiz videos as drafts to the operator's TikTok inboxes. No data is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored on a private server (Railway, European region) with restricted access, and are never published or committed to source code. They are refreshed automatically and replaced at each refresh.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the service is in use. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions); stored tokens are then deleted.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
